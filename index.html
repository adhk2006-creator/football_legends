Welcome

From a dusty street kick-about to a packed stadium under floodlights, football is the one game almost everyone on earth has played, watched or argued about. This site is a small tribute to the players who turned that game into something more — an art form, a source of national pride, and, for millions of kids like the ones in the photo above, a reason to run until the sun goes down.

Explore the photo gallery for a look at four of the game's most decorated players, read more about the project, or get in touch on the contact page. For the official record books, the links below are a good place to start.
